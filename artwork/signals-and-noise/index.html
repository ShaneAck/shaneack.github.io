Making Space: Signals and Noise was a two-part installation open to the public for one night only as part of Nuit Blanche Toronto 2026. The work was presented by Arts Etobicoke and hosted at the Humber Cultural Hub in Etobicoke, Ontario. Both installations invited visitors to collaborate in the construction of a dataset, challenging how scientists produce information and what stories we tell. If you participated in the work and made a note of the time, you may be able to find your contribution in the videos below.
